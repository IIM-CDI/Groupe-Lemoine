# Groupe Lemoine Website Redesign

**Project Overview:**  
**Groupe Lemoine** is a **European leader in cotton hygiene products**. Founded in 1978 by Philippe and Jeanne Lemoine, the company has grown into an international brand with over 900 employees, producing high-quality hygiene products and promoting sustainable practices. The group operates multiple production sites and is known for its commitment to innovation, quality, and environmental responsibility.



https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/192943cb-7c21-424e-9e5c-3c0659957aa8



The goal of this project was to **redesign and modernize the entire website** for Groupe Lemoine. Our team, consisting of **3 developers and 2 designers**, aimed to create a more interactive experience, improve the visual design, and focus on attracting talent through the Careers page.  

---

## **Objectives**

- Modernize the website design and user experience.  
- Make the site more interactive and visually appealing.  
- Highlight company achievements, products, and production sites.  
- Optimize the Careers page to attract talent and facilitate applications.  
- Migrate the backend from WordPress to Strapi for easier content management.  

---

## **Tech Stack**

- **TypeScript:** Ensures type safety and reduces runtime errors.  
- **Next.js:** Handles server-side rendering and routing efficiently.  
- **React:** Provides a dynamic and component-based UI structure.  
- **Tailwind CSS:** Enables rapid styling with utility-first CSS classes.  
- **Strapi:** Serves as the backend CMS, easier and more flexible than WordPress for managing content.  

---

## **Pages & Features**

- **Home Page (Accueil):**  
  - Full-screen **hero video** introducing the company.  
  - Highlights products, statistics, and company achievements.  
  - Interactive **world map** displaying production sites.  
  - Footer and header implemented in **layout.tsx** for consistency.  

- **Products Page (Produits):**  
  - Displays all products with **certifications** and detailed descriptions.  
  - Interactive UI to showcase categories and features.  

- **Production Sites (Sites de production):**  
  - Shows images and descriptions of different production locations.  
  - Users can explore each site and see related details.  

- **Careers (Carrière):**  
  - Filter job offers by **experience, contract type (full-time, internship, etc.), and work mode (hybrid, remote, etc.)**.  
  - Clicking “Apply” redirects to a **form** to submit CV and personal information.  
  - Form submissions send an **email to the HR team** automatically.  

- **CSR (RSE):**  
  - Highlights sustainability initiatives, **EcoVadis certifications**, and awards received.  

- **Contact Page:**  
  - Users can submit questions through a **contact form**.  
  - Includes **FAQ** section for common questions.  

---

## **Backend Migration**

- Migrated from **WordPress to Strapi** to simplify content management and provide more flexibility.  
- Strapi allows non-technical users to easily update products, news, and other content.  

---

## **Key Features**

- **Interactive components:** Hero video, statistics, map, and animations.  
- **Dynamic content fetching:** Uses Strapi APIs to load content for products, FAQs, and career listings.  
- **Email handling:** Submitting applications or contact forms triggers email notifications to the responsible teams.  
- **Responsive design:** Works on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.  

---

## **Team & Collaboration**

- **3 Developers:** Implemented frontend, backend, and integrations with Strapi.  
- **2 Designers:** Crafted the visual design, UI components, and ensured brand consistency.  
- Close collaboration to ensure design fidelity, performance optimization, and a seamless user experience.

This redesign project significantly improved the website's usability, modernized the look and feel, and enhanced content management for Groupe Lemoine’s team.
